Questions, answered.

So… what is it, exactly?

Burst candy built like a pre-workout. The outside is an extremely sour shell; the inside is a liquid energy core. Four pieces = one serving: 200mg caffeine, 200mg L-theanine, ~1g beta-alanine. Take it about 15 minutes before training — no shaker bottle required.

Is it safe? Who's it for?

It's made for healthy adults 18+. One serving has 200mg of caffeine — about a large cold brew — so skip it if you're under 18, caffeine-sensitive, or pregnant, and don't stack it with other caffeinated drinks or supplements. The beta-alanine tingle feels strange but is normal and harmless. As with any supplement, check with your doctor if you have a medical condition. Keep out of reach of children.
