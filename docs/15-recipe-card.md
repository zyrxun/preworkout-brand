# 15 — Recipe Card v1.4 + Flavor Lab (follow-along)

One batch = **30 pieces = 6 servings** (5 pieces per serving). Per piece: 40mg caffeine / 40mg L-theanine / **600mg beta-alanine (350mg core + 250mg shell-loaded)** / 350mg L-citrulline malate (shell).
Per serving (5 pieces): **200mg caffeine / 200mg L-theanine / 3.0g beta-alanine / 1.75g L-citrulline malate.**
Version notes: v1.4 — serving 3→5 pieces (batch 24→30), caffeine 40mg/piece (200mg/serving unchanged), BA shell-load 150→250mg (3.0g/serving), citrulline malate ADOPTED at 350mg/piece (1.75g/serving); weigh-list drift fixed (theanine 1.2g, BA core 10.5g, glycerin 10g, water-to-45g dose anchor); ORDER CORRECTED — shell cooks first, core second, assembly last (the core is the temperature-fragile component: caffeine + BA recrystallize as it cools, so it should go pot → pipette with zero cooling time; the shell holds happily in a warm bath for ~30 min). v1.3 — beta-alanine shell-loading. v1.1 — core malic 0.6g (pH ≥3.0, theanine stability); allulose banned (Maillard trap — docs/13). Reaction & compound-safety review: docs/20.

## Mise en place (10 min, do first)

- [ ] Scales on, calibrated (0.01g kitchen scale; 0.001g mg scale if you have it)
- [ ] Molds clean + fully dry (water ruins gummy set)
- [ ] Thermometer clipped to pot; strainer or coffee filter over a jar; large bowl of hot water = holding bath

## Part A — Shell base (~20 min; holds pourable ~30 min in a warm bath)

1. **Bloom now:** sprinkle **12.5g gelatin** over **37.5g cold water**. Wait 5 min — it turns into a solid brick. Correct. (1:3 ratio — looks too dry, isn't.)
2. **Wet-start the cook:** **62.5g sugar + 62.5g corn syrup + 15g water**, stir until fully dissolved *before* it boils. Then boil to **239–248°F (240–248°F)** — thermometer tip IN the liquid, not the foam (foam under-reads → overshoot → burn); lively simmer, not volcano; swirl constantly. No probe? Cold-water test: drip into cold water → **firm ball that holds shape but squishes = pull NOW**.
3. Off heat → cool to **~194°F (90°C)**, stir in the bloomed gelatin until fully melted. Skim foam.
4. Cool to **~176°F (80°C)**, stir in **2.5g citric acid** (malic 1:1 works) + **7.5g beta-alanine** + **10.5g L-citrulline** (plain citrulline swaps 1:1 for citrulline malate) (~90s, fully dispersed; powders ≈12% of shell weight is the gritty ceiling — if it pastes, cut citrulline by a third) + **shell flavor drops** + color.
5. **Working window: 158–176°F (70–80°C).** Hold the pot in a bain-marie — outer water boiled then **heat OFF**, nest the pot; re-warm the bath when the shell drops below ~150°F (tilt test: syrup should cross the pot bottom in ~2 seconds). Never a rolling boil under gelatin + acid (weak set). Pour/dispense in ONE fast pass; drizzle with a spoon pre-warmed in the bath, or a squeeze bottle that LIVES in the bath. Tilt + tap the mold so blobs level; below ~140°F the shell sets on contact.

## Part B — Core master syrup (~15 min; keep warm ~50°C)

1. Open **6 caffeine pills** over the pot, dump powder into **~20g hot water**. Stir till clear; strain (or let the binder settle and pour off the clear top) — **keep ALL the liquid**, rinse anything it touched with a spoonful of hot water back into the pot. Wipe spills immediately.
2. Warm (~50°C, never boiling): stir in **1.2g theanine** + **10.5g beta-alanine** until dissolved. Slight haze OK — no grains sitting on the bottom.
3. Off heat: stir in **10g glycerin**, **0.6g malic acid** (pH ≥3.0 guards the theanine), and **~⅔ of your flavor drops**.
4. **Weigh the pot: top up with hot water until the total reads exactly 45.0g.** This is the dose anchor — every active is dosed against 45g.
5. **Dose check:** 45g ÷ 30 = 1.5g/piece → caffeine 1200/45 × 1.5 = **40mg ✓** · theanine **40mg ✓** · BA **350mg ✓** (shell adds 250 → 600mg/piece ✓). Hold at ~50°C. Do not boil (theanine + flavor degrade); re-warm gently if the BA hazes.

## Part C — Assembly, two-pour (~30 min + 20–30 min fridge)

1. **First pour:** fill cavities ~40% with shell liquid — fast pass, tilt + tap the mold so it levels (mold on a warm surface helps). Fridge 3–5 min until a firm skin holds a puddle.
2. **Cores:** pipette **1.5g warm core** into each center — aim middle, don't smear the walls.
3. **Cap** with remaining shell liquid to the top. Fridge 20–30 min → **then rest the mold at room temp 10–15 min** (cold-demold = condensation = sticky).
4. Demold: push from the back of each cavity, don't yank.
5. **Cure: uncovered on a rack, 12–24h** (flip once halfway) — the surface skins over and stops sticking. This step is not optional.
6. **Batch QC:** weigh mold before cores and after — (after − before) ÷ 30 should be **1.5g ± 10%**. Log it.
7. Store airtight, fridge, **≤14 days**. Discard any weeper/leaker.

## Part D — Sour coat (day of eating)

- Toss pieces in: **2 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp malic + ½ tsp citric** (all-malic works).
- The coat dissolves into the gummy over days — coat only what you eat this session.

### Texture & citrulline notes

- **Texture target: SOFT-ish shell** — gives under your teeth, core **oozes** out (Gusher-soft, not hard-crack). Knob: less gelatin = softer shell (8g/batch = softer, 10g = medium; 12.5g = firmer cap). Hard isomalt shell is an optional later variant, not the target.
- **Citrulline honesty:** 350mg L-citrulline malate per piece = **1.75g/serving (~1.17g L-citrulline at 2:1)** — below the 6–8g clinical pump dose; position as pump support, never a clinical-dose claim. Stability mirrors beta-alanine (docs/20). Texture watch: if the shell reads gritty at taste-test, cut citrulline to 250mg/piece.

## Flavor Lab (week 3) — intensity ladder × 3 flavors in ONE batch

**Split, don't multiply:** one core syrup + one shell batch, split 3 ways. Drops go in OFF-HEAT.

1. Core: split the **45g** syrup into **3 × 15g cups** → **12 / 20 / 28 drops** per cup (light / medium / bold). Stir, keep warm.
2. Shell: split into **3 pans** → **4 / 6 / 8 drops** per pan. Three flavors (green apple / watermelon / blue raspberry) run the ladder inside each flavor.
3. Make the mold do the matrix: pour intensity-1 shells in column 1, intensity-2 column 2, intensity-3 column 3; deposit matching cores.
4. Coat split: 10 pieces per column **Low sour** (3:1 sugar:malic) vs **High sour** (1:1 sugar:malic).
5. Score each variant COLD (fridge temp = serving temp; cold mutes flavor ~20%):

**Score card (per variant):** Sour _/10 · Burst _/10 · Bitter _/10 (lower better) · Aftertaste _/10 · "Would you eat this pre-workout?" Y/N

- 5 tasters × 2 pieces per variant, water + plain cracker between, 30 min gaps
- Winner's drop count = baseline; next batch shift the whole ladder from that number (flavor is oil-soluble — drops never change the active dose math)
- Too-low flavor is recoverable (+5 drops next batch); too-high = oil leak / soft gummy, unrecoverable

## HALF BATCH — first-timer size (15 pieces = 3 servings)

Everything scales linearly; the dose anchor moves 45g → **22.5g total** (22.5 ÷ 15 = 1.5g/piece — per-piece doses identical, math verified):
- Core: 3 caffeine pills (600mg) in **20g hot water** (anchor absorbs evaporation) · theanine 0.6g · BA 5.25g · glycerin 5g · malic 0.3g · hot water to **22.5g total** · hold ~122°F, never on the flame after dissolving
- Shell — **FULL-SYRUP COOK (batch-1b fix, prevents small-batch scorching):** cook the whole **62.5g sugar + 62.5g corn syrup + 15g water (wet-start)** to 239–248°F, thermometer tip IN THE LIQUID not the foam; then **split the hot syrup in half by weight** — gelatin 6.25g (bloomed in 18.75g) + 1.25g malic (or citric) + 3.75g BA + 5.25g citrulline go into ONE half; reserve the other half (plain syrup) in a jar for the next attempt. Citrulline: plain L-citrulline swaps 1:1 for citrulline malate. Citric → malic 1:1.
- Bain-marie: boil the outer water → OFF heat → nest the pot; re-warm only when the shell drops below ~150°F. Never a rolling boil under gelatin+acid.
- Assembly/QC identical; QC ÷ 15 = 1.5g ± 10%. Flavor ladder: core 3 × 7.5g cups → 6 / 10 / 14 drops; shell 3 pans → 2 / 3 / 4 drops.
- Don't go smaller than half: tiny sugar batches scorch before the thermometer reads, and sub-0.5g weighs drown in scale error.

## Kitchen learnings (batch 1a/1b — read before cooking)

- **Core water can be microwaved** (45–60s, steaming = done; tap cup before reaching in if no bubbles). ⚠️ NEVER re-microwave/reheat the core after the 22.5g (or 45g) anchor weigh — evaporation concentrates the dose. Re-warm = sit it in the bath only.
- **Overshot the anchor?** Don't dump it: new deposit = 1.5 × (actual total ÷ target total). At 27g → deposit 1.8g/piece → dose exact. QC catches it.
- **Core = water-thin is CORRECT.** No gelatin in it; it's a liquid burst center. Slightly thicker than water from glycerin, that's all.
- **Undercooked syrup** (pulled below 239–248°F) = wet, sticky, pours badly. Overcooked = hard gummy. The knob is the pull moment: firm-ball, immediately.
- **Hard gummies?** Drop gelatin: 12.5g → 10g (full) / 6.25g → 5g (half) = softer, Gusher-ooze target.
- **Small-batch scorch fix:** cook the FULL 62.5+62.5 syrup even for a half batch, split by weight (tare working pot → pour → read), reserve the other half in a jar.
- **Reserve syrup rules:** plain syrup only, jarred. It hardens glassy — hot-water soak 10–15 min (or open jar, microwave 10–15s bursts) returns it to pourable. **Per 10g of reserve syrup: 1g gelatin · 0.2g malic · 0.6g BA · 0.8g citrulline.**
- **Bain-marie:** outer water boiled → heat OFF → nest; re-warm bath below ~150°F. Rolling boil under gelatin+acid = weak set.
- **Foam under-reads probes** — tip in the liquid. Dial thermometers lag 10–20°F; digital instant-read or the cold-water test.

## Safety rails (non-negotiable)

- Caffeine by capsule count only — never loose powder, never re-dried extract
- Count ALL caffeine that day (coffee counts). Cap 400mg/day total
- Self-test ramp: day 1 → 1 piece; day 3 → 2; day 5 → 3; day 7+ → full 5-piece serving, once daily
- Stop if palpitations/anxiety/insomnia. Not if under 18, pregnant, heart conditions, stim meds
- Rinse after eating (acid); don't brush for 30 min
