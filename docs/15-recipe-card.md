# 15 — Recipe Card v1.3 + Flavor Lab (follow-along)

One batch = **30 pieces = 6 servings** (5 pieces per serving). Per piece: 40mg caffeine / 40mg L-theanine / **600mg beta-alanine (350mg core + 250mg shell-loaded)** / 350mg L-citrulline malate (shell).
Per serving (5 pieces): **200mg caffeine / 200mg L-theanine / 3.0g beta-alanine / 1.75g L-citrulline malate.**
Version notes: v1.4 — serving 3→5 pieces (batch 24→30), caffeine 40mg/piece (200mg/serving unchanged), BA shell-load 150→250mg (3.0g/serving), citrulline malate ADOPTED at 350mg/piece (1.75g/serving). v1.3 — beta-alanine shell-loading (shells suspend powder easily, no solubility limit). v1.2 — serving 4→3 pieces. v1.1 — core malic 0.6g (pH ≥3.0, theanine stability); allulose banned (Maillard trap — docs/13).

- [ ] Scales on, calibrated (mg scale w/ calibration weight; pocket scale tared)
- [ ] Molds clean + fully dry (water ruins gummy set)
- [ ] Thermometer clipped to pot; coffee filter set in a funnel over a jar

## Part A — Core master syrup (~15 min)

1. Open **6 caffeine pills** over the pot, dump powder into **22.2g hot water**. Stir till clear; strain through the coffee filter (catches binders). Wipe spills immediately.
2. Off heat, stir in **1.2g theanine** + **10.5g beta-alanine** until dissolved (warm helps; slight haze OK — dissolve fully before the glycerin goes in).
3. Stir in **20g glycerin**, **0.75g malic acid**, and **~30 drops** of your flavor (split flavors AFTER step 5 in the Flavor Lab variant).
4. Total should read **≈45g** on the pocket scale — top up with water if short.
5. Keep warm (~50°C, not hot). **Do not boil** (theanine + flavor degrade). Beta-alanine may haze as it cools — re-warm gently.
6. **Dose check:** 45g ÷ 30 = 1.5g/piece → 1.5 × (1200/45) = **40mg caffeine ✓** (BA: 10500/45 × 1.5 = **350mg ✓**)

## Part B — Shell base (~20 min)

1. Bloom **10g gelatin** in **30g cold water**, 5 min. It becomes solid — that's correct.
2. In the second pot: **50g sugar + 50g corn syrup**, cook to **115–120°C (240–248°F)**. No stirring once boiling; swirl only. *(Hot sugar = bad burns. No kids/pets in the kitchen.)*
3. Off heat → cool to **~90°C** (2–3 min), stir in bloomed gelatin until fully melted. Skim foam.
4. Cool to **~80°C**, stir in **2.5g citric acid** + **7.5g beta-alanine** + **10.5g L-citrulline malate** (v1.4 shell-loads — stir ~90s until fully dispersed; powders ≈12% of shell weight is the gritty ceiling — if it pastes, cut citrulline to 7.5g) + **~18–25 drops** flavor + color. Keep pot in a warm water bath so it stays pourable ~20 min.

## Part C — Assembly, two-pour (~30 min)

1. **First pour:** fill cavities ~40% with shell liquid. Fridge 3–5 min until a skin sets firm enough to hold a puddle.
2. **Cores:** pipette **1.5g warm core** into each center — aim middle, don't smear the walls.
3. **Cap** with remaining shell liquid to the top. Fridge 20–30 min. Demold.
4. **Batch QC:** weigh mold before cores and after — (after − before) ÷ 24 should be **1.5g ± 10%**. Log it.
5. Store airtight, fridge, **≤14 days**. Discard any weeper/leaker.

## Part D — Sour coat (day of eating)

- Toss pieces in: **2 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp malic + ½ tsp citric**.
- The coat dissolves into the gummy over days — coat only what you eat this session.

### Texture & citrulline (v1.3)

- **Texture target: SOFT-ish shell** — gives under your teeth, core **oozes** out (Gusher-soft, not hard-crack). Knob: less gelatin = softer shell (8g/batch = softer, 10g = medium). Hard isomalt shell is an optional variant, not the target.
- Total per piece: 350mg BA (core) + 250mg BA (shell) = **600mg → 3.0g/serving (5 pieces)**. Shell suspension has no solubility limit — texture is the only cap (~12% w/w total shell powders at v1.4 — right at the working ceiling).
- **Citrulline (ADOPTED v1.4):** 350mg L-citrulline malate per shell piece = **1.75g/serving (~1.17g L-citrulline at 2:1)**. Honest caveat: still below the 6–8g clinical pump dose — position as pump support, never a clinical-dose claim. Stability mirrors beta-alanine (amino acid, Maillard suppressed by the low-pH core, shelf-life docs/13). Texture watch: at taste-test, if the shell reads gritty, cut citrulline to 250mg/piece.

## Flavor Lab (week 3) — 3 flavors × 2 sour levels in ONE batch

**Split, don't multiply:** make ONE core master syrup and ONE shell batch, then split.

1. Core: after step 4, split the 36g syrup into **3 × 12g cups**. Into each: **15–17 drops** of one flavor (green apple / watermelon / blue raspberry). Stir, keep warm.
2. Shell: split into **3 pans**, **5–6 drops** matching flavor each.
3. Make the mold do the matrix: pour flavor-1 shells in column 1 of the mold, flavor-2 column 2, flavor-3 column 3. Deposit cores to match.
4. Coat split: after demolding, 10 pieces per flavor get **Low sour** coat (3:1 sugar:malic, 2 tsp blend) and 10 get **High sour** (1:1 sugar:malic, 2 tsp blend).
5. Score each variant cold (fridge temp = serving temp):

**Score card (per variant):** Sour _/10 · Burst _/10 · Bitter _/10 (lower better) · Aftertaste _/10 · "Would you eat this pre-workout?" Y/N

- 5 tasters × 2 pieces per variant, water + plain cracker between, 30 min gaps
- If a flavor reads weak: bump +5 drops next batch (flavor is oil-soluble — adding drops never changes the active dose math)
- Winner + winning sour level → becomes the v2 single-flavor full batch

## Safety rails (unchanged, non-negotiable)

- Caffeine by capsule count only — never loose powder, never sun-dried extract
- Count ALL caffeine that day (coffee counts). Cap 400mg/day total
- Self-test ramp: day 1 → 1 piece; day 3 → 2; day 5+ → 4 max, once daily
- Stop if palpitations/anxiety/insomnia. Not if under 18, pregnant, heart conditions, stim meds
- Rinse after eating (acid); don't brush for 30 min
