# Unit Economics & Startup Cost (illustrative estimates)

All numbers are planning estimates to be replaced with real manufacturer quotes — flag anything you build on these.

## COGS (contract-manufactured, at modest scale)

| Line | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Gummies, 120/bottle (30 servings) | $2–5 |
| Bottle + label + desiccant + seal | $0.60–1.20 |
| Supplement Facts panel + artwork | amortized |
| 3rd-party CoA testing (per batch) | $300–800/batch |
| **Landed COGS/bottle (ex-freight)** | **≈ $3–7** |

## Pricing & margin

| | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| SRP | $29 | $39 |
| COGS | $7 | $3 |
| **Gross margin (before ads)** | **~76%** | **~92%** |

Compare: powder pre-workout at $1/serving and $0.30 COGS looks better on paper — but gummies command the 1.5–2.9x price premium (docs/01-market.md), which is the entire reason the format wins on margin.

## The real cost: CAC

Supplement DTC lives and dies on paid social. Blended CAC for new supplement brands typically runs **$20–40+**; subscriptions are the only thing that makes LTV work. Plan:

- Subscription price ~$25/mo (Jüced-style) → payback in 1–2 orders required
- TikTok Shop + organic creator seeding to dilute paid CAC
- Amazon as a "high-intent catcher" (people already searching "pre workout gummies")

## Startup cost scenarios

| Scenario | What | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| **Validation (do this first)** | Landing page + waitlist + $500–1k TikTok/Meta creative test + manufacturer samples | **$1–2k** |
| First run, low-MOQ | 500–2,500 bottles/bags from a low-MOQ manufacturer (Nutrikal ~500 bottles; Matsun ~2,500 gummies; Handian ~1,000 gummies; Your Gummy Vitamins ~500 units) + labels/design/CoA | $5–15k |
| First run, **liquid-center burst format** | Fewer specialists (co-extrusion / SiMoGel-type lines, several Asia-based); higher MOQs and longer lead times | **$10–25k** |
| Traditional MOQ | 100k–200k gummies/run (Nutracap and most established players) | $30k+ — **don't start here** |

## Break-even sketch

- 1,000 bottles at $29 = $29k revenue, ~$22k gross profit
- Against $8k inventory run + $6–10k launch ads → break-even around first-run sell-through if subscription attach ≥ 25%

## Kill criteria (be honest before spending)

- Manufacturer quote > $9/bottle landed at low MOQ
- Ad test CAC > $45 with no subscription interest
- Landing page waitlist conversion < 10% from click
