## Shortlist guidance
- **Brand-father pick:** 01 (Dead King) — carries the hero art, most ownable
- **Product-story pick:** 06 (Core Section) or 04 (Shatter) — explains burst candy instantly
- **Minimal/app-icon pick:** 03 (Thorn Mark) or 08 (Pierced Profile)
- **Merch/stamp pick:** 09 (Seal) — doubles as a packaging sticker
