## 1. Product spec sheet (send this)

**Working title:** BRANDNAME — chewable burst-candy energy (pre-workout positioning)

**Format:** two-component candy piece. Firm gummy or hard shell (sour-coated) + sealed liquid center (~35–40% of piece weight) that releases on bite.

**Target piece weight:** 4.0 g (shell ~2.5 g + liquid core ~1.5 g) · **Serving:** 3 pieces (12 g)

**Label claims per serving (3 pieces):**
- Caffeine anhydrous 200 mg (~67 mg/piece)
- L-theanine 200 mg (1:1 with caffeine)
- Beta-alanine ~1,050 mg (350 mg/piece)
- Sugar 0 g* (sugar-free target)

**Core (liquid fill):** glycerin/water system, target water activity **aw < 0.65**, pH **3.0–3.5** (do not go below 3.0 — theanine stability), caffeine + theanine + beta-alanine dissolved, natural flavor. Preservative: potassium sorbate 0.05–0.1%.

**Shell:** gelatin (v1) or pectin (target) gummy OR isomalt-based hard shell. Sweetener system: **isomalt/maltitol + stevia — no allulose, no reducing sugars adjacent to the amino-acid core** (Maillard control, see docs/13).

**Sour system:** dry coat or encapsulated malic acid (encapsulated strongly preferred for shelf life; free malic fades within weeks).

**Stability target:** 12–18 months ambient. Key risks to engineer out: core→shell water migration, seam leaks, theanine hydrolysis (pH), browning near amino acids.

**Packaging:** 30-serving child-resistant jar + 12-count single-serve pouches. **Child-resistant closure is mandatory** (caffeine product).

**Compliance:** FDA-registered, cGMP (21 CFR 111) facility required. Per-batch CoA (caffeine/theanine/beta-alanine potency + micro + heavy metals), Prop 65 documentation, Supplement Facts panel support.

**Commercial ask:** MOQ, price per bottle at 500 / 1,000 / 5,000 units, lead time, sample policy, liquid-fill technology used (co-extrusion vs injection vs starch-mold half-fill), and whether you can match the pH/aw spec above.
