# 20 — Reaction & Compound-Safety Review (v1.4 recipe, batch-1 procedure)

Scope: every ingredient vs our exact process — caffeine pills dissolved in hot water, core held ~50°C (never boiled), shell sugar cooked to 115–120°C, amino acids stirred in off-heat at ≤80°C for ~90s, gelatin bloom + melt, 14-day fridge storage in gummy matrix. Compiled 2026-11-05.

## Verdict: GREEN — no hazardous compound formation at our temps, pH, or times. The real risks are physical (burns, weep, mold), not chemical.

## Per ingredient

**Caffeine (6 pills → core).** Extremely stable in water: thermal degradation only begins ~130–160°C (dry/coffee-roast territory); sublimates ~178°C. Our max exposure ~50–60°C aqueous for minutes → retention ~100%. Does not hydrolyze in acid, does not react with amino acids at food temps. Pill binders (MCC, magnesium stearate) are inert food-grade; strain/settle them out.
⚠ Process note: caffeine solubility is temperature-dependent (~2.2g/100mL at 25°C vs ~18g/100mL at 80°C). 1200mg in ~20g water fully dissolves ONLY hot — keep the core ≥50°C from dissolve to deposit, use ALL the liquid, never discard any of it.

**L-theanine (1.2g → core).** Documented degradation pathways need either pH ≤2 (~5%/24h at pH 2 — our core is calibrated pH ≥3.0 via 0.6g malic) or ≥120°C for 2h (dry-heat products: N-ethylformamide, succinimide etc. — none reachable in aqueous ≤60°C). The 8-week Arrhenius storage study shows 4°C = slowest degradation; tea-plant hydrolase (pH 8.5 optimum) is not present in purified powder. At our pH/temp/storage: expected retention ≥97% over 14 days.

**Beta-alanine (10.5g core + 7.5g shell).** Simple beta amino acid — among the LOW-reactivity amino acids in Maillard systems (same family as alanine/glycine). Main chemical risk is recrystallization as the core cools (physical, reversible by re-warming — not a safety issue). Physiological paresthesia (tingling) is a sensory effect, not damage.

**L-citrulline malate (10.5g → shell).** More stable at neutral-to-slightly-acidic pH (our shell pH ~3 — within its comfort zone); dry decomposition >200°C. 90s at 80°C then set = negligible loss. Malate is a food acid (found in apples); no interaction with gelatin or sugars beyond normal acid effects.

**Maillard browning (theanine + BA + citrulline × corn syrup reducing sugars).** Reactants present, but rate needs heat + time + neutral-alkaline pH. We have: amino acids in off-heat at ≤80°C for ~90s, acidic shell (2.5g citric — acid slows Maillard strongly), fridge storage at 4°C, 14-day window. Expected outcome: no browning to imperceptible tint by day 14. This is why the cook sequence matters — NEVER cook the amino acids in the syrup (that was the allulose v1.1 trap, docs/13).

**Sugar + corn syrup cooked to 115–120°C.** Below caramelization (~160°C) and below HMF/acrylamide formation territory for our system (acrylamide needs asparagine + reducing sugar >120°C — no asparagine-containing ingredients in the formula). Inversion (sucrose→glucose/fructose) from the shell acid is intentional/beneficial (texture).

**Gelatin (12.5g → shell).** Bloom + melt ≤90°C is standard. ⚠ Quality note: gelatin + acid + prolonged heat hydrolyzes the gel (weak/soft set) — always add the acid+powders at ≤80°C off-heat and cap/pour promptly; never boil after gelatin is in.

**Malic acid (0.6g core + coat).** Food acid (apples); its job in the core is pH ≥3.0 for theanine stability. Glycerin + malic esterification requires strong acid + high heat + long times — negligible at 50°C/minutes.

**Vegetable glycerin (10g → core).** Humectant; inert at our temps. Also solvent support for caffeine.

**LorAnn flavor oils (off-heat only).** Food-grade; added ≤80°C / ~50°C precisely because oils are volatile (top notes boil off — quality loss, not safety).

## Hazards that are REAL in this procedure (all physical, not chemical)
1. 115–120°C sugar syrup burns — worst injury risk in the recipe. Swirl don't stir, no kids/pets.
2. Caffeine hygiene — pills only, wipe spills, count total daily caffeine (400mg cap incl. coffee).
3. Storage — no preservatives: 14-day fridge rule, discard any weeper/mold/off-smell.
