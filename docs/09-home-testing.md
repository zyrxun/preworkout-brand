# Home Testing — Feasibility, Recipe, Self-Test Protocol

## Verdict: can you make it at home?

**Prototype-grade YES — product-grade NO.**

- ✅ At home you can validate: shell texture, burst mechanics, flavor system, sour level, tingle feel, dose feel, and film all the TikTok content.
- ❌ At home you can never get: shelf-stable leak-proof seals, guaranteed dose uniformity, GMP/compliance, or legal sellability (cottage-food laws don't cover caffeinated supplements). Home batches are R&D + content, never product.

## V1 recipe — "sour shell, energy core" gummy (24 pieces = 6 servings)

Per piece: ~2.5g shell + ~1.5g liquid core = **50mg caffeine / 50mg L-theanine / 250mg beta-alanine**.
Per serving (4 pieces): **200mg caffeine / 200mg theanine / ~1g beta-alanine.**

### Equipment

- 0.001g milligram scale (for theanine/beta-alanine weighs) + 0.01g kitchen scale (deposits)
- 2× 50-cavity silicone mini molds, syringes **or** plastic transfer pipettes/droppers (pipettes are disposable — easier than washing sugar syrup out of glass droppers), thermometer (clip-on candy OR digital instant-read meat probe — keep the tip mid-liquid, off the pot bottom; analog dial oven thermometers are too slow/drift), small pots
- Deposit-precision trick: weigh the mold before and after filling all cores — total ÷ 24 should be ~1.5g (±10% is fine)

### Core master syrup (makes 24 cores, 36g total)

1. Caffeine source: **6 × 200mg pills** — capsules: open them and dump the powder into **20g hot water** (tablets work too, just drop in). Stir until fully dissolved; binders/flow agents may leave sediment — **strain through a coffee filter** and keep the clear liquid. Dosing is accounted by capsule count, so no powder weighing needed. Never dose loose powder directly or dry the extract back into powder (bitterness + hazard) — the caffeine lives in the liquid core, where it's masked. Open capsules over the pot and wipe spills immediately (keep powder away from kids/pets).
2. Stir in **1200mg L-theanine** and **6000mg beta-alanine** until dissolved (warm helps; slight haze OK).
3. Add **16g vegetable glycerin**, **0.6g malic acid** (v1.1 — was 1.5g: keep core pH ≥3.0, theanine hydrolyzes ~2x faster below pH 3; the sour punch lives in the coating), **~½ tsp LorAnn flavor** (green apple first).
4. Total ≈ 36g. Keep warm (~50°C) — beta-alanine can recrystallize as it cools.
5. **Dose check:** 36g ÷ 24 pieces = 1.5g/piece → 1.5g × (1200mg/36g) = 50mg caffeine ✓. Weigh every deposit on the 0.01g scale; log it.

### Shell base (gelatin gummy, covers 24 pieces)

1. Bloom **10g gelatin** (2.5 Knox packets) in **30g cold water**, 5 min.
2. Cook **50g sugar + 50g corn syrup** to ~115–120°C, remove heat.
3. Stir in bloomed gelatin until melted; skim foam. Cool to ~80°C.
4. Stir in **2g citric acid** + a few drops of flavor/color.

### Assembly (two-pour)

1. Fill cavities ~40% with shell liquid. Set 5–10 min (or fridge 3–5 min) until firm enough to hold a puddle.
2. Weigh **1.5g warm core** into each center (pipette/syringe) — don't touch the walls.
3. Cap with remaining shell liquid; fridge 20–30 min; demold.
4. Sour coat day-of eating: **2 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp malic acid + ½ tsp citric acid**, tossed on. (Coating dissolves into the gummy over days — known issue, documented in docs/08.)
5. Store airtight in fridge. **Consume within 14 days** (no preservatives; discard on any bloom/mold/smell).

### Flavor matrix for week 3

3 flavors (green apple / watermelon / blue raspberry) × 2 sour levels (coat: 1 tbsp vs 2 tbsp malic) = 6 variants, 4 pieces each. Score cards: burst intensity 1–10, sour 1–10, aftertaste, "would you eat pre-workout."

### Stage 2 (week 4+): isomalt hard-shell version

Melt isomalt (~150°C, burns are serious — gloves, no kids/pets around), cast half-shells in silicone, fill cooled cores, seal with melted isomalt cap. True Warheads crack. Expect leaks within weeks at home — good enough to test texture/ sensationalism, not shelf life.

## Self-test protocol (caffeine safety rules)

- **Pills/tablets only, never bulk caffeine powder** (FDA explicitly warns consumers off it).
- Count ALL caffeine sources that day (coffee, energy drinks). **Cap: 400mg/day total.**
- Day 1: 1 piece (~50mg). Note onset at 15/30/60 min, jitters vs smooth, heart rate.
- Day 3: 2 pieces (~100mg). Day 5+: 4 pieces (~200mg) ~20 min pre-workout.
- Expect: theanine smoothing the edge; beta-alanine face/hands tingle at 10–20 min (harmless).
- Stop and don't retry if: palpitations, anxiety spiral, insomnia. Don't test if under 18, pregnant, heart conditions, or on stimulant meds. One test serving/day, never re-dose.
- Dental: rinse water after; don't brush for 30 min (acid softens enamel).

## What "success" looks like after ~4 weekends

- 6-variant matrix scored; winning flavor + sour level identified
- Deposit-mass log showing ±10% caffeine consistency (feasible with pills + weigh-deposits)
- Photo/video of the burst = landing page + ads material
- A one-page spec sheet to hand co-mans: target piece weight, core composition, actives/serving, flavor/sour targets
