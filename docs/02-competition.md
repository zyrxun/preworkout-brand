# Competitive Landscape

## Benchmark (US, DTC + Amazon)

| Brand | Format | Caffeine/serving | Price/serving | Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jüced** | Gummies (4/serving) | ~95mg | ~$1.46–2.86 (sub → one-off) | "Pre-workout for the girls" — peach, L-Carnitine + Cognizin + L-Theanine; 100K+ reviews, 4.8★ |
| **SAVOR** | Gummies | — | ~$1.00 | Best-tasting per Garage Gym Reviews; budget-friendly |

| **Bucked Up** | Chocolate-covered gummies | — | — | Incumbent powder brand extending into format |
| Powder incumbents (C4, Gorilla Mode, Transparent Labs, Ghost) | Powder | 150–350mg | $0.50–1.00 | Full clinical doses; what gummies *cannot* compete with on performance |

## Key takeaway from the benchmark

**No gummy brand competes on dose — and the successful ones don't try.** Jüced at ~95mg caffeine sells an experience (taste, ritual, girl-math aesthetic), not a pump. The moment you claim "replaces my pre-workout powder," reviewers dock you for the physics (see docs/03-product.md).

## Whitespace

1. **Niche rituals:** Jüced took "for the girls." Open niches: climbers/runners/cyclists (lighter stim, portability matters), college gym crowd, gaming/esports-adjacent focus gummies, golf ("range gummies" pattern is proven in other categories).
2. **Stim-sensitive segment:** most powders are 200–350mg; a clean 75–150mg "smooth energy" gummy (caffeine + L-theanine) targets the half of gym-goers who find pre-workout powders too janky.
3. **Sugar-free / pectin / vegan:** most gummies still gelatin-based; vegan pectin + high melt-point formula is a differentiator and solves summer shipping.
4. **Flavor as marketing:** one loud signature flavor (Jüced = electric peach) beats a flavor matrix.

## Threat: incumbent entry

Bucked Up already has gummies; C4/Ghost could follow with distribution advantages. Defense: niche community ownership + subscription DTC before the big brands notice the niche.
