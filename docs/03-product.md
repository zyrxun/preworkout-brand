# Product: Formulation Reality Check

## The physics problem (read this before designing the product)

A "real" pre-workout powder serving contains:

| Ingredient | Clinical dose | Fits in a gummy? |
|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | 200–350mg | ⚠️ Only across 4+ gummies (bitter; ~25–75mg per gummy is the taste ceiling) |
| Beta-alanine | 3.2g (tingles) | ❌ You'd need a fistful of gummies |
| L-citrulline malate | 6–8g (pump) | ❌ Physically impossible in candy format |
| Creatine | 3–5g | ❌ Same volume problem |

A gummy weighs ~3–4g and can hold a few hundred mg of actives before texture/taste breaks. **Conclusion: a gummy will never replace a scoop.** Every market-leading gummy pre-workout accepts this and sells 75–150mg caffeine + light nootropics.

## Recommended product concept

**"Chewable pre-workout energy" — 4 gummies per serving:**

- Caffeine ~100mg/serving (25mg per gummy keeps bitterness manageable)
- L-theanine ~100–200mg/serving (smooths caffeine edge — the classic 1:1–1:2 ratio)
- Optional: L-carnitine, tyrosine, or citicoline for a focus story (small doses, label-friendly)
- Pectin-based (vegan-friendly + higher melt point for shipping)
- Sugar-free or low-sugar (allulose/stevia) — gym audience expectation
- 1 loud signature flavor launch (electric peach / sour green apple class), not a flavor line
- 30-serving bottle = 120 gummies, SRP $29–39 ($0.97–1.30/serving — undercuts Jüced one-off pricing)

## What NOT to claim

- Not "replaces your powder pre-workout" (you will get review-bombed on dose)
- No disease/medical claims (FDA/FTC — see docs/05-risks-compliance.md)
- Structure/function only: "supports energy, focus, endurance*"

## Positioning statement (draft)

> Pre-workout you can chew. Toss a pack in your bag, chew 4 on the walk in, go.
> Smooth energy, no shaker, no chalk, no jitters.

Format ideas: single-serve pouch packs (gym-bag/travel + TikTok-friendly) alongside the bottle; pouches also enable sampling/seeding.
