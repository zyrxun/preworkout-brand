# Market Analysis

## Category size & growth

Research firms disagree on absolute size but agree on direction. All figures below are from their public report summaries (2025 baseline):

| Segment | 2025 | Forecast | CAGR | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-workout gummies | $0.85B – $1.7B | $3.1–4.9B by 2033 | 8.7–23.8% | Grand View, Transpire, Spherical Insights |
| Caffeine gummies (all use cases) | $126M | $276M by 2031 | 13.9% | TechSci Research |
| Gummy supplements (all types) | $11.75B | — | — | Fortune Business Insights |
| Pre-workout supplements (all formats) | ~$18B (2026) | $29.2B by 2035 | 6.1% | Business Research Insights |

Read: gummy supplements are an $11.75B category with North America holding ~44% share — the gummy format has already won on consumer preference (easier, tastier, no shaker). Pre-workout is the highest-spend-per-user corner of sports nutrition, and its gummy slice is early: estimates vary 2x between firms, which itself signals a young, fast-moving category.

## Demand proof

- **Jüced** (category leader, peach energy gummies): **100K+ reviews at 4.8★**, priced ~$2.38/day, sells a "pre-workout for the girls" positioning via L-Carnitine + Cognizin + L-Theanine + caffeine.
- **Garage Gym Reviews** (major fitness outlet) now maintains a dedicated "best pre-workout gummies" roundups — the format has earned its own review category.
- **Bucked Up** (major powder brand) ships chocolate-covered pre-workout gummies — incumbents are validating the format with their own SKUs.
- **Animal** sells performance chew tabs at $0.93/serving — budget end is covered too.

## Why this works (consumer logic)

1. **Convenience premium:** no scoop, no shaker, no chalk taste, no mixing — chew 4 gummies on the way to the gym.
2. **Taste-led repeat purchase:** gummies are candy-first; flavor is a moat powders don't have.
3. **Gym-bag portability / travel:** powders are a TSA and gym-bag hassle.
4. **Non-gym expansion:** same product sells as a "coffee replacement" / study energy — the caffeine gummy market ($126M, 13.9% CAGR) is adjacent and cross-sellable.

## TAM sanity check for a new entrant

Even the conservative read (Transpire: $0.85B → $4.9B by 2033 at 23.8% CAGR) implies the category is adding hundreds of millions in annual spend while young. A new brand needs a rounding error of this market (0.01% ≈ $85k–170k/yr revenue) to prove the business.
