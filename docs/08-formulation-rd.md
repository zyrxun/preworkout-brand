# Formulation R&D — Literature Findings (v1)

How a caffeine + L-theanine + beta-alanine burst candy could actually work, grounded in candy-science sources.

## The three actives — solubility & stability verdicts

| Active | Target/serving (4 pieces) | Solubility reality | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | 200mg (50mg/piece) | ~2.2g/100mL water (RT); heat-stable at candy temps (sublimes ~180°C, no degradation in syrup work) | ✅ Fits in the liquid core easily at our doses |
| L-theanine | 200mg (50mg/piece) | Moderately soluble, dissolves in hot water/syrup; mild umami taste, easily masked | ✅ Fits in the core |
| Beta-alanine | ~1g (250mg/piece) | **54.5 g/100 mL water** (Wikipedia) — huge headroom; may recrystallize as cold glycerin-heavy syrup cools → deposit warm, slight grit acceptable in v1 | ✅ Fits in the core at the tingle dose; the 3.2g clinical dose still doesn't fit |

## Construction: how liquid-center candies are actually made

- **Commercial:** liquid-filled candy machines co-extrude/inject core into moving candy ropes or half-shells (Dhiman Group process overview); a classic patent (US5626896A) half-fills jelly candy in starch molds, adds the core, then caps. Industrial but the *concept* is simple: shell → core → cap.
- **Home-feasible (r/CandyMakers consensus):** two methods —
  1. **Layered two-pour:** fill mold cavities ~40% with gummy base, let set, weigh in liquid core, cap with more base. ← chosen for v1
  2. **Hollow shell + syringe fill + cap.**
- Amazing Food (molecular gastronomy blog) confirms silicone molds + syringe-injected liquid cores work at home.

## Shell system options

| System | Texture | Home difficulty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gelatin gummy (bloomed + cooked sugar/corn syrup) | Chewy burst (Gusher-like) | Easy | v1 choice. Classic ratios: bloom gelatin in cold water; cook sugar+corn syrup to ~115–120°C; stir gelatin in below ~85°C; add acid/flavor at the end |
| Isomalt hard candy shell | True Warheads crack | Medium (hot sugar, ~150–170°C, burn risk) | Sugar-free, low hygroscopicity — the professional sugar-free hard candy base. **Stage 2** after gummy version works. Moisture from the core eventually leaks through home-made seals — fine for testing, not shelf life |
| Pectin gummy | Chewier, vegan | Medium (needs acid/calcium set, trickier texture) | Later optimization; also solves vegan + melt-point |
| Spherification (popping boba) | Membrane burst | Easy but useless for dosing/shelf | Demo/content only, not a product path |

## Flavor & sour system (how flavors go in)

- **Flavors:** LorAnn Super-Strength oils — concentrated for candy work, heat-stable in hot syrup (that's what hard-candy makers use). Base flavors to test: **green apple, watermelon, blue raspberry** (~1/4–1/2 tsp per pound of candy; scale to taste). Flavor goes into BOTH layers: most in the core, a little in the shell.
- **Sour:** Warheads-grade sour = **malic acid** (sharper, longer-lasting than citric). Classic home coating: ~3:1 sugar:citric; r/CandyMakers favor **2:1 citric:malic in the mix**; Warheads-level → weight toward malic. v1 coat: 2 tbsp allulose/sugar + 1 tbsp malic + ½ tsp citric.
- **Known coating failure:** the powder dissolves into the gummy over days (moisture migration) — apply day-of, or accept fading sour. Commercial fixes use encapsulated acids (future formulation item for the co-man).
- **Sweetener:** sugar + corn syrup works for v1 kitchen tests; allulose/stevia versions are the v2 target (gym-clean label + less enamel double-hit).

## Safety double-check of the formula

- Per piece 50mg caffeine → a toddler eating one piece gets a bad afternoon, not an ER visit; per serving 200mg = ~2 cups of coffee, under the FDA's 400mg/day adult guideline.
- Beta-alanine ~1g/serving triggers the tingle (harmless paresthesia) — must be disclosed on any label (see docs/05).
- Malic/citric acid load: rinse-after guidance; enamel note in docs/05.
- Kitchen hygiene: syrup high in water = mold risk without commercial water-activity control → refrigerate, airtight, 14-day horizon for all home batches.
- **Never bulk caffeine powder at home** — the FDA explicitly warns consumers off pure powdered caffeine (lethal in teaspoon doses, unmeasurable on kitchen scales). This plan uses **200mg tablets only** for dose-safe handling.

## Price & doability double-check

- First experimentation kit: **~$150–170** all-in from Amazon (docs/11) — covers ~150–200 test pieces.
- Per-piece ingredient cost at home ≈ $0.06–0.10 (actives dominate: beta-alanine + theanine ≈ $0.05/piece) — consistent with commercial COGS estimates in docs/04 once materials hit bulk pricing.
- Doability verdict: **prototype-grade at home = yes; sellable product at home = no** (shelf stability, dose uniformity, GMP, and cottage-food laws all point to a co-man — that path is docs/05–06).
